Our Story

Started with one gap we couldn't ignore

In 2018, a small group of coders in Kampala noticed something troubling — Ugandan schools were teaching about computers, but almost nobody was teaching students to build with them. Mindset Coders started as an answer to that gap: affordable, hands-on technology education that treats every learner as a future creator, not just a future user.

What began as informal coding sessions has grown into a nationwide movement reaching thousands of students and teachers across Uganda, backed by an official seat at the table for global programs like the World Robot Olympiad.

Our Vision

To create a future where every young person in Africa can access quality STEM education, develop future-ready skills, and become a creator of solutions that improve lives and communities.

Our Mission

To build accessible STEM learning pathways that connect young people, educators, schools, communities, and industry through innovative learning experiences, mentorship, competitions, and technology-enabled opportunities.
