Our Story

Started with one gap we couldn't ignore

In 2018, a small group of coders in Kampala noticed something troubling. Ugandan schools were teaching about computers, but almost nobody was teaching students to build with them. Mindset Coders started as an answer to that gap: affordable, hands-on technology education that treats every learner as a future creator, not just a future user.

What began as informal coding sessions has grown into a nationwide movement reaching thousands of students and teachers across Uganda, backed by an official seat at the table for global programs like the World Robot Olympiad.

Why STEAM, not just STEM

The move from STEM to STEAM is intentional. The Arts connect technology with creativity, culture, design, music and storytelling. At the 2026 World Robot Olympiad, the theme was "Robots Meet Culture", and students used robotics alongside music, art and cultural expression. That is the kind of learning we want to promote.

Technology does not replace creativity. It gives creativity new ways to come alive.
Our Vision

To create a future where every young person in Africa can access quality STEAM education, develop future-ready skills, and become a creator of solutions that improve lives and communities.

Our Mission

To build accessible STEAM learning pathways that connect young people, educators, schools, communities, and industry through innovative learning experiences, mentorship, competitions, and technology-enabled opportunities.
