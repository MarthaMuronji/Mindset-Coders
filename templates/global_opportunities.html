Learning that reaches beyond Uganda

Mindset Coders connects learners to a wider global STEAM ecosystem, through competitions, peer learning and the chance to show their work to the world.

Ways to take part

WRO is one doorway, and there are more

International competitions

STEAM and robotics competitions where learners test what they have built against peers from other countries.

World Robot Olympiad

Uganda's top teams represent the country at the WRO finals. We have been Uganda's official WRO affiliate since 2020 and run the national qualifiers.

Technovation Girls

We lead Technovation Girls in Uganda's Central region. Girls build mobile apps to solve problems in their communities, connecting Ugandan learners to an international network of young innovators.

Africa Code Week

We led Africa Code Week in Uganda from 2019 to 2022.

Peer to peer learning

Learners collaborate with students in other countries.

Showcase your work

Chances to present learner projects and innovations to a global STEAM community.

Flagship program

WRO Uganda National Championship

As Uganda's official World Robot Olympiad affiliate, we run the national qualifiers that send our top learners to compete on the global stage.

How competing works

Regional and national rounds

School teams build and program robots to solve a themed annual challenge.

Challenges through the year

Shorter, themed hackathon-style challenges give learners a lower-stakes way to build competition experience.

Getting ready

We help schools and learners form a team, prepare and register for the next qualifying round.
