What is WRO Uganda?

WRO Uganda is the national version of the World Robot Olympiad. Students solve robotics challenges that follow international standards, and the best teams move up from school to regional to national level. Anyone can take part. Moving up is based on merit. Mindset Coders runs WRO Uganda nationally.

How the pathway works

1

1. Identification

Students take on structured robotics challenges at school and regional level.

2

2. Selection

The strongest teams go through to the national finals, judged to the same standards for everyone.

3

3. Acceleration

Top teams move on to regional and global competitions.

4

4. Continuity

Through university innovation hubs, top students keep developing their ideas after the competition.

Who it is for

Schools

Students

Sponsors and partners

Help young talent in Uganda get noticed and grow, including students from schools with fewer resources.

Fair to every school

WRO Uganda works to include students from schools with few resources by removing cost barriers, without lowering the standard of the competition. Talent is everywhere. Opportunity is not.

After the competition

Top students can get mentorship from industry professionals, access to advanced facilities and research centres, and support turning their projects into real innovations.
